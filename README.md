## whoami

I read Industrial & Systems Engineering at **IIT Kharagpur**, class of 2028, and I run operations at
[**Amoh**](https://amoh.ai), the medical AI research lab. The rest of my time goes where statistics meets
markets: forecasting, factor research, optimisation, and the occasional 3 a.m. contest problem. Right now I am
working through stochastic calculus, econometrics, and market microstructure.

I advise the **Kharagpur Data Analytics Group**, having run it as Executive Head the year before.

<td valign="top">

Frontier models pass medical exams and still stall on the actual work: committing to a diagnosis, choosing what to
rule out first, admitting when the evidence is thin. **Amoh** builds the environments, evals and feedback traces that
train models on hard cases from real clinics, verified against what was feasible for each patient, with 10+ clinic
partners across the US. I run operations. Plenty to read. Very little to practise.
